Company Internal Policies Manual

1. Code of Conduct

1.1 Professional Behavior
• Maintain respectful communication with all employees.
• Avoid abusive or discriminatory language.
• Consequence: First violation: Written warning
• Consequence: Repeated violations: Suspension up to 5 days
• Consequence: Severe misconduct: Termination

1.2 Integrity and Ethics
• Disclose conflicts of interest.
• Do not engage in bribery or corruption.
• Consequence: Immediate investigation
• Consequence: Possible termination and legal action

2. Attendance and Working Hours

2.1 Working Hours
• Standard working hours: 9 hours per day.
• Core availability: 10 AM – 5 PM.
• Consequence: Non-compliance: Salary deduction
• Consequence: Repeated issue: Performance warning

2.2 Attendance
• More than 3 unapproved absences in a month is unacceptable.
• Late login beyond 30 minutes more than 5 times/month is a violation.
• Consequence: Warning after threshold breach
• Consequence: Leave deduction
• Consequence: Escalation to HR

3. Data Security

3.1 Confidential Data Handling
• Do not share company data externally.
• Use only approved storage systems.
• Consequence: Immediate access revocation
• Consequence: Termination
• Consequence: Legal proceedings if breach is severe

4. Use of Company Assets

4.1 Acceptable Use
• Devices must be used only for work.
• Unauthorized software installation is prohibited.
• Consequence: Device access restriction
• Consequence: IT disciplinary action

5. Anti-Harassment Policy
5.1 Workplace Harassment
• Includes verbal, physical, or online harassment.
• Applies to all employees and stakeholders.
• Consequence: Immediate suspension during investigation
• Consequence: Termination upon confirmation
• Consequence: Legal reporting if required

6. Leave Policy
6.1 Leave Limits
• Casual Leave: 12 days/year
• Sick Leave: 10 days/year
• Earned Leave: 15 days/year
• Consequence: Excess leave: Salary deduction
• Consequence: Unauthorized leave: Disciplinary action

7. Disciplinary Actions Framework
7.1 Escalation Levels
• Level 1: Verbal Warning
• Level 2: Written Warning
• Level 3: Suspension
• Level 4: Termination
• Consequence: Actions depend on severity and repetition

8. Compliance
8.1 Legal and Policy Compliance
• Employees must follow all applicable laws.
• Mandatory policy acknowledgment annually.
• Consequence: Non-compliance may lead to termination
• Consequence: Legal liability where applicable
